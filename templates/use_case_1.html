Use Case 1

Predicting Student Dropout Risk in Online Courses

Problem context

An online education platform wants to identify, early on, which students are at risk of dropping out of a course, so instructors can reach out and offer support before it's too late. Online courses often have high dropout rates because instructors can't easily notice when a student starts disengaging, and by the time a student stops submitting assignments altogether, it is usually too late to intervene effectively.

Data that could be used

Login frequency, video lesson completion rate, quiz scores, assignment submission rate, forum participation, and days since the student's last activity on the platform.

What should the model predict?

For each student, the model should predict whether they are "at risk of dropping out" or "likely to continue" the course, ideally with a probability score, early enough in the course for a meaningful intervention.

Type of Machine Learning that could apply

Supervised learning, since the platform has historical records of students who finished their courses and students who dropped out. This is a classification problem.

Potential benefit

By identifying at-risk students early, instructors can send personalized reminders, offer extra support, or adjust deadlines before the student disengages completely, improving course completion rates and student retention.
