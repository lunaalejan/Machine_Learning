Use Case 4

Early Detection of Anomalies in Chest X-Rays

Problem context

Hospitals receive large volumes of chest X-rays every day, and specialists have limited time to review each one individually. This can delay urgent diagnoses, especially in busy emergency departments where a small but critical fraction of images shows a real abnormality that requires immediate attention.

Data that could be used

Thousands of historical chest X-rays already reviewed and labeled by radiologists (normal / abnormal), along with the clinical notes describing the final diagnosis for each case.

What should the model predict?

For each new X-ray, the model should classify the image as "normal" or "possible anomaly," and ideally flag the region of the image where the finding appears, so a radiologist can review it quickly.

Type of Machine Learning that could apply

Supervised learning, since labeled examples reviewed by specialists are already available. This is a classification problem.

Potential benefit

Prioritizes the most urgent cases for human review, speeds up diagnosis, and reduces radiologists' workload without replacing their judgment.
