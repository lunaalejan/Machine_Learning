Naive Bayes Model Evaluation

This section presents the results obtained by the Naive Bayes model using the customer dataset. The main classification metrics are analyzed and the results are compared with the Logistic Regression model.

These metrics allow us to evaluate how well the model classifies customers according to their probability of making a purchase.

Naive Bayes vs Logistic Regression

The following table presents the real results obtained during the evaluation of both classification models.

Metric Logistic Regression Naive Bayes
Accuracy 72.50% 80.00%
Precision 76.19% 76.92%
Recall 72.73% 90.91%
F1-Score 74.42% 83.33%

Comparison result: Naive Bayes achieved better overall performance than Logistic Regression across all four evaluated metrics. It achieved an Accuracy of 80.00%, a Precision of 76.92%, a Recall of 90.91%, and an F1-Score of 83.33%. The most significant result is the Recall, where Naive Bayes correctly identified 90.91% of the customers who actually made a purchase. Based on these results, Naive Bayes provides the best performance for this dataset.

Model Characteristics

Logistic Regression

Logistic Regression is a classification model that estimates the probability of an observation belonging to a specific class. In this project, it is used to classify whether a customer will make a purchase or not.

Naive Bayes

Naive Bayes is a classification algorithm based on Bayes' theorem. It assumes independence between the variables used and calculates the probability that a customer belongs to a specific class.

Evaluation Process

1. Data Preparation

The independent variables and the target variable were selected from the dataset.

2. Data Splitting

The dataset was divided into 80% for training and 20% for testing.

3. Model Training

The Gaussian Naive Bayes model was trained using the training dataset.

4. Prediction

The model generated predictions using the testing dataset.

5. Evaluation

Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and the confusion matrix were calculated.

6. Model Comparison

Finally, the results were compared with the Logistic Regression model.

Variables Used

  • Age: customer's age.
  • Web Visits per Month: number of visits made by the customer.
  • Time on Site: amount of time the customer spends on the website.
  • Discount Used: indicates whether the customer used a discount.
  • Target: indicates whether the customer made a purchase.

Final Interpretation

The results show that Naive Bayes was the model with the best performance on this dataset. Its Accuracy of 80.00% indicates that it correctly classified most of the testing observations. In addition, its Recall of 90.91% demonstrates a high ability to identify customers who actually made a purchase. Its F1-Score of 83.33% also shows a good balance between Precision and Recall. In comparison, Logistic Regression obtained lower results across all four evaluated metrics.
